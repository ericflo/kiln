# H79 H64/H77 TIES D20

## Hypothesis

H78 trained a mixed corpus that combined H64's real repair-continuation signal
with H77's moderate-thinking anchor. It won the small smoke but failed the
wider gate. H79 tested whether the issue was destructive interference during
training rather than the two directions themselves.

The experiment used the server's TIES adapter merge to combine the already
trained H64 and H77 adapters at equal weight with density 0.2. This trims each
adapter to high-magnitude parameters, elects a sign per parameter, and keeps
only sign-consistent values. If H64 and H77 carried complementary signal, TIES
could preserve that signal while dropping conflicting low-magnitude noise.

## Verdict

Rejected at smoke.

TIES did remove enough interference to avoid zero rollouts, but it did not
beat a strong paired base draw and it added substantial latency. This rules out
a simple H64/H77 weight-space blend as the stabilizer H78 was missing. The next
useful direction should not keep reusing H64 repair-continuation plus H77
moderate-thinking as the main signal; it needs either a broader behavior
anchor, a different real-reward source, or an OPD/teacher-style signal that
adds capability rather than reweighting known fragile adapters.
