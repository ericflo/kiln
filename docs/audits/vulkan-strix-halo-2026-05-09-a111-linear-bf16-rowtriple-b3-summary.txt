A111 rejected candidate: Vulkan packed-BF16 generic linear row-triple at batch 3

Scope
-----
- Temporarily added `linear_decode_batched_rows3_bf16w.comp`.
- Routed packed-BF16 generic `linear_decode` batch exactly `3` through the
  row-triple shader.
- Temporary rollback env:
  `KILN_DISABLE_VULKAN_LINEAR_BF16_ROWS3=1`.
- CUDA and Metal source paths were not changed.
- Final source was reverted after measurement; only this audit trail remains.

Why
---
- Metal E464 accepted a batch-3 transposed GEMV row-triple tile8 path.
- Vulkan A110 made batch-3 live decode-batcher traffic a meaningful shape to
  isolate.
- A036 already rejected a direct generic row-pair port, so this was treated as
  a narrow candidate requiring clear live evidence.

Validation While Present
------------------------
- `cargo fmt --check`
- `git diff --check`
- `cargo check -p kiln-vulkan-kernel`
- Focused Vulkan parity:
  `cargo test -p kiln-vulkan-kernel --test gdn_parity linear_decode_batched_bf16_packed_weights_match_cpu_reference -- --nocapture`
  covering batch 3 and batch 4 while present.
- Focused rollback parity with
  `KILN_DISABLE_VULKAN_LINEAR_BF16_ROWS3=1`.
- Full Vulkan kernel parity: `34 passed`.
- `cargo check -p kiln-model --features vulkan`
- `cargo check -p kiln-server --features vulkan --bin kiln --bin kiln-bench`
- `cargo build --release -p kiln-server --bin kiln --features vulkan`

Live Batch-3 A/B
----------------
- Server env for valid probes:
  `KILN_MODEL_PATH=Qwen3.5-4B KILN_DECODE_BATCH_WAIT_US=5000`
- Rollback env additionally:
  `KILN_DISABLE_VULKAN_LINEAR_BF16_ROWS3=1`
- The old live decode-batcher path was used; `KILN_BATCHING_ENGINE=1` was not
  used for these measurements.
- Pair 1: candidate `1.566030s`; rollback `1.566556s`.
- Pair 2, counter-order with fresh prompts: rollback `1.909421s`;
  candidate `1.881894s`.
- Every valid arm had identical counters:
  `requests_ok=3`, `tokens_generated=9`, `jobs_submitted=6`,
  `worker_batches=3`, `batcher_rows=6`, `max_batch_after=3`,
  `jobs_failed=0`.
- Pair 1 visible outputs matched:
  `"The sequence you"` for all three streams.
- Pair 2 visible outputs matched: `"26,"`, `"36,"`, `"46,"`.
- Two-pair average: candidate `1.723962s`; rollback `1.737988s`.
- Candidate average win: `0.014027s`, about `0.8%`.

Verdict
-------
- Rejected and reverted.
- The candidate was correct, but the live win was within noise and not large
  enough to justify another generic linear decode shader.
- Do not retry generic packed-BF16 row grouping for Vulkan by direct Metal
  mirroring without a stronger, Vulkan-specific scheduling or residency
  hypothesis.
