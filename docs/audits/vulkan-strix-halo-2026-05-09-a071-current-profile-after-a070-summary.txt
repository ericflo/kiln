Interpretation
--------------

- Prefill is still dominated by the Vulkan MLP fused path, followed by GDN
  recurrent, GDN in-proj, GDN conv, and full-attention QKV projection.
- Decode keeps the same concrete ordering at the top: MLP fused first, then
  GDN recurrent/in-proj, then GDN out-proj/gated-norm/gates and full-attention
  QKV/O projection.
- The recent Metal MLP SiLU/multiply fusion does not directly transfer to this
  Vulkan profile because the Vulkan MLP fused bucket is much wider than only
  the pointwise SiLU/multiply operation and still includes CPU/GPU boundary
  effects around the current backend tensor shape.
- The recent Metal GDN A/B projection and QKV/Z prefill-combine experiments do
  not directly transfer either: Vulkan GDN prefill already uses a custom
  in-proj route that emits q/k/v/z/a/b products together.

Verdict
-------

Keep as target-selection evidence only.

The next Vulkan work should prioritize reducing MLP boundary/data movement or
changing GDN prefill/decode residency around recurrent/in-proj/conv. Do not
default-enable the corrected A070 full-chunk shader without changing its
parallelization strategy, and do not spend more time on direct Metal tile ports
unless an isolated Vulkan profile shows that the same inner operation, not the
host/device boundary, is the limiting factor.

No CUDA or Metal source path changed.
