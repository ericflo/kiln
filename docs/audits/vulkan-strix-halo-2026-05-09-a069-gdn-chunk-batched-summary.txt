Verdict
-------

Rejected and removed before commit.

The profiled recurrent substage improved, but the fresh no-profile A/B did not
show a default-safe prefill win: candidate prefill was 2188.749211 ms versus
rollback 2173.140301 ms. The candidate did improve mean decode ITL in that
fresh pair, but this experiment targets prefill chunk movement and does not
justify taking a default prefill regression risk.

Do not retry this by simply batching the current chunk prep/scan transfers.
Future GDN prefill work should reduce the CPU boundary/readback shape or make
the prep/scan products resident across adjacent prefill stages.

No CUDA or Metal source path was changed by the final committed state.
