A076: Reject MLP Row-Pair Gate/Up 64x4 Tile

Date: 2026-05-09
Target: Qwen3.5-4B, Vulkan backend, AMD Radeon 8060S Graphics (RADV_STRIX_HALO)

Purpose
-------

A074 showed the 64-token MLP prefill bucket is dominated by
`gate_up_dispatch`. A075 showed direct widening from row-pair `64x2` to
`128x2` did not confirm under no-profile A/B. A076 tested the other obvious
axis: keep the row-pair output tile width at `64`, but split the hidden
dimension across `4` reduction lanes instead of `2`, inspired by llama.cpp's
Vulkan matvec emphasis on parallel reductions.

Temporary Change
----------------

Temporarily changed `mlp_gate_up_decode_batched_rows2.comp`:

- `local_size_y`: `2 -> 4`
- shared partial arrays: `128 -> 256`
- partial index: `col_lane * 2 + red_lane -> col_lane * 4 + red_lane`
- hidden loop stride: `h += 2 -> h += 4`
- final reduction: sum four lanes instead of two lanes

The workgroup count and output-column grouping stayed unchanged.

Validation
----------

Candidate validation:

- `cargo test -p kiln-vulkan-kernel --test gdn_parity mlp_decode_batched_matches_cpu_reference -- --nocapture`
- `cargo build --release -p kiln-server --bin kiln-bench --features vulkan`

Final reverted-source validation:

- `cargo fmt --check`
- `git diff --check`
- `cargo check -p kiln-vulkan-kernel`

No-Profile A/B
--------------

No-profile candidate and rollback command:

`KILN_BENCH_LOG_TOKENS=1 ./target/release/kiln-bench --model-path
Qwen3.5-4B --paged --latency-only --latency-warmup-runs 1 --prompt-tokens 64
--max-output-tokens 8 --seed 76 --quiet`

No-profile candidate, temporary `64x4`:

- Token IDs:
  `[271,1206,1423,680,1204,1691,51864,3520,506]`.
- Prefill: 2486.717907 ms.
- Mean ITL: 98.524340375 ms.
- P50 ITL: 98.287638 ms.
- P99 ITL: 104.499858 ms.

No-profile rollback after restoring `64x2`:

- Token IDs:
  `[271,1206,1423,680,1204,1691,51864,3520,506]`.
- Prefill: 2280.474615 ms.
- Mean ITL: 98.743757875 ms.
- P50 ITL: 98.422814 ms.
- P99 ITL: 100.756925 ms.

The candidate lost the measured prefill comparison by `206.243292ms`.

Verdict
-------

Reject and revert. Keep the existing row-pair MLP gate/up `64x2` tile.

Do not retry the direct `64x4` row-pair gate/up tile without a new hypothesis.
More reduction lanes increased overhead enough to lose the same-harness
no-profile comparison.
