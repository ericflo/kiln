Vulkan Strix Halo A083 summary, 2026-05-09

Experiment:
- Temporarily added a Vulkan `causal_conv1d_prefill_k4` shader inspired by the
  Metal conv prefill layout.
- The candidate used one workgroup per `(batch, channel)` stream, computed all
  output timesteps, then advanced `conv_state` after a workgroup barrier.
- Rollback env during the trial:
  `KILN_DISABLE_VULKAN_CONV1D_PREFILL_FUSED_STATE=1`

Validation while temporary source was present:
- `cargo fmt --check`
- `cargo check -p kiln-vulkan-kernel`
- `cargo check -p kiln-model --features vulkan`
- `cargo test -p kiln-vulkan-kernel --test gdn_parity causal_conv1d_prefill_matches_stateful_cpu_reference -- --nocapture`
- `cargo build --release -p kiln-server --bin kiln-bench --features vulkan`

Correctness:

Verdict:
- Reject and remove the temporary source.
- The Metal one-workgroup-per-channel conv prefill shape does not transfer to
  the current Vulkan Strix Halo prefill path as a default.
- Final source contains no A083 Vulkan shader or dispatcher changes, and CUDA
  and Metal source paths are untouched.
