# Hypothesis: h1-r16-6ep

## Family
H1 — epoch curve (first ablation)

## Claim
Running OPD with the proven recipe (rank=16, alpha=32, lr=1e-4, 6 epochs,
samples_per_prompt=1, max_tokens=128, top_k=8) will lift `symbol_precision`
by ≥3pp (from 0.804 → ≥0.834), with no regression on other sub-scores.

## Mechanism
The 4B's failure mode (listing imported symbols as defined ones) is exactly
the kind of "student samples in distributions the teacher considers off-by-one"
that OPD bridges. At sampling time, when the student emits `EventEmitter`
after listing the defined names, the teacher assigns it low probability;
reverse-KL pushes the student away from emitting it. The supervision is dense
at every token of the listed-names span.

- prompts: datasets/train.opd.jsonl (40 prompts)
- rank: 16, alpha: 32, lr: 1e-4
- epochs: 6, samples_per_prompt: 1, max_tokens: 128, top_k: 8
- env: KILN_STREAMING_PREFILL=1

## What's held constant from previous iteration
N/A — first OPD iter for this capability. Starting from the v7-proven recipe

## Falsification plan
- If `symbol_precision` Δ < +1pp: H1 has no signal at 6 epochs; either the
  recipe doesn't transfer to this capability (unlikely, similar shape to
  JSON-schema) OR the rubric saturates around 0.80 due to a structural
  4B/27B alignment issue. Try epoch=9 then bail to H2 (more prompts).
- If `symbol_precision` Δ ≥ +1pp but composite Δ < 0: a sub-score regressed
  (likely `symbol_recall` if the model becomes too conservative). Pivot to H7
  (Stable-OPD mixture) to anchor against drift.
- If `symbol_precision` Δ ≥ +3pp (matches claim): bump to 9 epochs in iter 2.
